Lab repository

Synthetic teaching data

All documents, reports, and CSV files in this package are invented for COSC-651. The service is called LedgerLine. It imports invoice records and produces a validation report. No real customer data or production service is involved.

Development cases D01 through D10 form the initial experiment. D11 through D15 expand the diagnostic set. Development answers are intentionally available for transparent scoring. Five additional cases are held by the instructor until the Week 8 evaluation. A fixed set of five cases gives limited coverage; students must state that limitation.

Documentation has a version field. Application version 2 must not use archived version 1 policy. Filenames are identifiers within a supplied fixture directory, not arbitrary filesystem paths.
